One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist — written to make a bored commuter smile on the way to work. The entry for the make.fun bounty "Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't".

The forecast:

> In Drowsywick, expect light rain and 12°C, with a 90% chance your bus is late — pack an umbrella and lower your expectations accordingly.

Drowsywick does not exist. The rain is real, the bus is late, and the only sensible advice is to lower your expectations.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (137 characters, deterministic — the same line on every run).
